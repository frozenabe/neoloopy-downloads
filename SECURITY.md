# Security Policy

If you believe you've found a security vulnerability in neoloopy, please report
it **privately** — do not open a public issue.

- Open a private advisory:
  https://github.com/frozenabe/neoloopy-downloads/security/advisories/new
- Or email **hello@neoloopy.com** with details and steps to reproduce.

We'll acknowledge your report, investigate, and keep you updated on a fix.
Please give us a reasonable window to address the issue before any public
disclosure. Thank you for helping keep neoloopy users safe.
